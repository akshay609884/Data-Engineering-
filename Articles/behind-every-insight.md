# Behind Every Insight, There Is Data Engineering

## Introduction

Data engineering provides the foundation for reliable
data analysis and informed business decisions.

## The Data Engineering Workflow

Business Activity → Raw Data → Data Pipelines →
Reliable Analytical Datasets → Analysis and Dashboards →
Informed Business Decisions

## Business Activity and Raw Data

Business activities generate raw data from transactions,
customers, operations, and other business processes.

## Data Pipelines

Data pipelines collect, process, clean, and transform
raw data into a usable format.

## Reliable Analytical Datasets

Data quality checks and validation help ensure that
datasets are consistent and suitable for analysis.

## Analysis and Dashboards

Analysts use reliable datasets to create reports,
visualizations, and dashboards.

## Informed Business Decisions

Accurate and timely insights help businesses make
better-informed decisions.

## Conclusion

Data engineering connects raw business data with
meaningful insights by building reliable data pipelines
and analytical datasets.
